Move
W A S D
You play one player the whole match, the ringed number 11. Hold Shift to sprint, but it drains your stamina bar. Stand at the net to block.
Pass
E hold, release
Sends the ball to the teammate your mouse points toward. Aim for the green notch on the bar. A third touch becomes a free ball to the crosshair.
Attack
Space hold, release
Hits it over the net. A fuller bar is harder, but past the red zone it can go out. Aim with the mouse: the ball lands where the crosshair is. The arrow shows where the hit will go.
Kick
F tap
Reaches much further than a normal touch, low balls only, and sends the ball to the crosshair. It only works 35% of the time, otherwise you miss.
Serving: aim with the mouse, hold Space for a power serve, hold E for a safe one. Press P to pause, Enter to skip a replay.
